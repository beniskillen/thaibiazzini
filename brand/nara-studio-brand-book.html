01 — The studio

Proof

the brand.

NARA Studio works with founders whose public presence is weaker than the work. The method starts with the people already closest to a decision: customers, followers, referrals, prior buyers, and the names that should hear from them first.

Map that market. Activate the warmest relationships. Track what moves. Then turn the evidence into positioning, content, and the next campaign. The brand has a commercial job.

02 — Wordmark

The mark
is the word.

NARA

Set NARA in Clash Display Semibold, uppercase, with open letter-spacing. There is no symbol. Use it in off-white on near black, or in near black on off-white. Leave clear space around it at least the width of the N.

Do not redraw it, outline it, put it in a box, or set it in another typeface. Studio sits in the sentence around the name, not inside the mark.

07 — In use

Black for
the argument.
Paper for
the work.

Open a page on near black when the founder is making a claim. Move to off-white when the page is explaining the method, the services, or a case. Keep photography full-bleed and grayscale when it is a portrait. Let the type sit on it, not in a chrome frame.

The wordmark stays NARA. The red stays on the next action. If a layout needs a third typeface or a fourth colour, it is no longer this brand.
