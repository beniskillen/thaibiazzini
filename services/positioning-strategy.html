What it defines

Before creating more content, the brand needs a message worth repeating. Positioning strategy creates the foundation for how your market should understand, remember and refer you.

  • Your ideal audience and buyer context.
  • The problem, category or opportunity you want to be associated with.
  • Your core message and point of view.
  • The proof and credibility angles that support trust.
  • The content pillars that keep the brand focused.
[ 02 ]

What you receive

Statement

Audience

Audience and buyer clarity that connects the message to real commercial context.

Message

Voice

Voice, tone, brand story and credibility angles that make the positioning feel like you.
